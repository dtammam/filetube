Downloads

Downloader engine

FileTube downloads videos with the yt-dlp engine. The bundled engine ships inside the app image and always remains as the fallback. The stable and nightly channels install the newest yt-dlp release at runtime - nightly tracks YouTube's changes closest. Every installed engine must pass a health check before it is used, and an engine that breaks later automatically reverts to bundled - the bell reports it either way.

Off by default. FileTube rings the bell with the result either way.

Notifications

Alerts for new downloads.

These apply to everyone on this FileTube.

Download bell

Adds a notification bell to the top-right corner that counts new subscription and one-off downloads, YouTube-style — tap one to watch it. The bell appears once at least one subscription exists. Turning this off hides the bell for everyone on this server; downloads are still tracked in the background, so turning it back on shows the full history with no gap. On by default.

Push notifications

Pushes each new download to your subscribed devices even when FileTube is closed. Turning it off pauses pushes to ALL your devices without forgetting them. Each device also has to be enabled once, below. On iPhone/iPad this works only for the home-screen-installed app (Share, then Add to Home Screen), and only over HTTPS.

Trash

Deleted videos move to a per-folder trash instead of being erased. Restoring brings back watch history, likes and queue spots exactly as they were.

Critters

Completely optional and purely for fun: little critters - or characters, or anything with a transparent background - peek out from behind cards and menus around the app, never over the video or audio player. Tap one for a little noise. Saved on this device. Drop your own transparent images into the public/critters folder and they join in automatically.

Off: critters never overlap at all (the default). On: they may lightly overlap each other, so you get more of them in tight spots. They still won't pile into a heavy stack - just the occasional light graze.

Off: each critter without its own sound file keeps one consistent borrowed sound. On: those critters play a random sound from your collection every tap. Critters paired with their own same-named sound file always play that.

Critter pool

The pool is server-wide - everyone's critters come from here. Upload transparent images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF); a sound with the same name (pearl.png + pearl.mp3) becomes that critter's tap voice. Names do not have to match: a critter without its own sound borrows one from the folder and keeps that same borrowed voice everywhere (the note badge below marks a critter's OWN sound). Deleting is permanent - Download all first if you want a backup.

Troubleshooting

Diagnostics for when something on THIS device misbehaves. Nothing here changes how FileTube works - these tools only observe and report. All device-local.

Error log

FileTube always keeps the last 50 script errors in this browser, so a page that will not load can be fixed. Export error log shares them as a text file (or copies them where file sharing is missing). If Settings itself will not open in Safari, open /errors.html in a Safari tab instead. An app added to the Home Screen keeps its own log, separate from Safari's: only its own Settings > Export error log can reach it. Kept on this phone or computer, never sent anywhere.

A player event log for diagnosing player issues (a black picture, background audio not engaging). Switch it on, then use the app as usual: it records in the background, across reloads and a force-quit, with nothing on screen. Come back here and press Export log to share it as a text file (or copy it where file sharing is missing). Clear log asks first. "Show the log on screen" adds a small panel at the bottom of the screen. Device-local, never sent anywhere.

A test for the black picture on the phone: a tap on the picture still pauses and plays, but the play/pause sign is not drawn over the video. Takes effect at once; device-local.

The rotate debug log records what the screen does when you turn the phone (and when the keyboard comes up in the Music player), frame by frame, in a small green panel at the bottom. Tap the panel to copy the rows. Takes effect at once, no reload; device-local.

Experimental

Features that are still earning their keep - sharp edges, battery costs, and platform quirks disclosed on each one. All device-local unless a setting says otherwise.

Keeps a video's audio playing when you switch apps or lock your screen -- mobile only. Off by default.

Makes background audio dependable: new downloads get their audio track extracted immediately, and extracted tracks are kept (not evicted from the cache) while this is on. Costs roughly 1 MB per minute of video on disk, and while on, the cache size cap governs video transcodes only (kept audio is excluded). Pairs with "Background audio for video" above.

Shows an Attribute button on unattributed videos and a bulk "attribute this folder" tool, for libraries with older downloads filed under the wrong channel. A library that was clean from the start never needs this. Off by default.

Shrinks the brief audio gap when you lock the screen during a video: the background audio track quietly keeps pace with your position while you watch, so the switch-over doesn't have to fetch. Costs occasional extra network requests during mobile video playback. Pairs with the two settings above.

When a video's audio is playing in the background, this plays a silent loop to keep the app awake so pausing from your AirPods or the lock screen can be un-paused again. Fixes the "pauses but won't resume when locked" quirk - but the app never sleeps while backgrounded, so it uses more battery. Leave off unless you want it. Per device; takes effect on the next video you open.
