## 1. Outcome (Dean's words, 2026-10-09)

"I spend too much time on things like Reddit... it would be cool to have a mode, like a feed mode where books, news articles,
maybe some short content videos I could go through in a feed, like a very TikTok style feed... the idea would be to get more
semi-educational brain rot style consumption out of one's local files... Nothing's here by accident. So I think it would be
kind of interesting and fun to be able to consume what you very deliberately got in a somewhat mindless way."

The device pass checks:

1. On the phone, Feed opens to a length picker (10 / 20 / 30 min), then an endless vertical swipe feed of cards from HIS library:
   the next pages of a book he is reading, a few minutes of a podcast, a chapter of a video, a Watch later item, a song.
2. What he does there is real progress: pages read move his bookmark, a podcast slice moves his place, a watched chapter counts.
3. When time is up it winds down on the current card and shows a recap of where the time went; "Another 10 minutes" is possible
   but deliberate and shows in the recap.
4. The success measure (Dean's, outside the app): Reddit time in iOS Screen Time goes down. The feed keeps its own session history
   so the two can be compared.

## 3. Kickoff defaults (D1-D14: state each in the ledger as built; Dean overrules at the device pass)

- **D1 Where.** A new top-level page `/feed`, phone first. An optional bottom-bar item "Feed" (in the bar's customizer, ON by
  default on phones) and a sidebar entry. Desktop shows the same feed as a centred phone-width column. Prefer the cheaper shell
  route the research found (serve an existing shell with its view swapped, like `/cleanup`: lib/cleanup/shell.js, server.js
  ~3500) over a new HTML shell if it carries the bottom bar correctly; measure and say which.
- **D2 The card stack.** Full-height cards, vertical scroll-snap, one card "active" at a time (the one ≥ 60% on screen). Only the
  active card plays; a card that leaves pauses. Swipe up = next, down = back (the session's own history). No infinite DOM: keep
  about 5 cards mounted.
- **D3 The mix.** `GET /api/feed?count=&exclude=` returns a batch of cards built server-side from the viewer's VISIBLE items only
  (lib/auth/visibility.js through the server.js wrappers: bookVisibleTo, podcastEpisodeVisibleTo, mediaVisibleTo,
  trackVisibleTo). Default weights: book 30, video 30, podcast 20, Watch later 10, song 10; a type with nothing to show drops out
  and its weight spreads; never the same type twice in a row unless only one type exists; never the same item twice in one
  session unless the library runs out (then say "You're through everything new" and recycle).
- **D4 Book card (EPUB only in v1).** Source: books "currently reading" (`GET /api/books?filter=reading` logic, routes.js:263),
  newest first, then liked unstarted books. A new server route returns about 450 words of PLAIN TEXT paragraphs from the saved
  position (locator spineIndex + blockIndex) using the TTS pipeline's pieces (`zip.extractEntryByName` + `chunkChapter`,
  lib/media/transcode.js:486-489, lib/books/tts-chunk.js:111; `resolveTtsChapter` server.js:1706), crossing into the next spine
  item when a chapter ends. Text only, rendered as text (never HTML). Card shows the book title, chapter, the excerpt, "Open in
  reader". PDFs are left out of v1 (no server text; disclosed). **Progress (R3):** when the card was active long enough to read
  it (words / 300 per minute x 0.6) and the user moves on, write the new position (spineIndex, blockIndex of the next unread
  block) through the existing progress path. **W1 must measure first** that read.js resumes correctly from a locator with
  spineIndex + blockIndex and no fresh CFI (or derive a CFI server-side); if the reader cannot honour it, STOP and ask.
- **D5 Bookmark safety (data loss surface: "I cannot lose data").** The feed only ever moves a book position FORWARD, never back;
  it never writes if the stored position changed after the card was served (another device read on); the previous locator is
  kept in the session record so a wrong move is recoverable. Same rule for podcast and video positions.
- **D6 Podcast card.** Episodes in progress (`filter=recent-listening`, lib/podcasts/index.js:1021) first, then the newest
  unplayed downloaded episodes from his subscriptions. Plays a 4-minute slice from the saved position (from 0 for a new one):
  the show art, title, a progress line; audio continues through the slice and the card says "4 min" left; swiping on pauses.
  Progress through the existing POST /api/podcasts/progress (forward only, D5).
- **D7 Video card.** In-progress videos (user_progress, 0.5-90%) and new-from-subscriptions items (lib/home/feed.js's selector)
  first. Plays ONE chapter (resolveItemChapters, server.js:2435) from the saved position's chapter; with no chapters, a 3-minute
  segment. Muted autoplay is NOT used; the active card plays with sound (it is a deliberate session). Host: the one shared player
  via `FileTube.player.load(id, data, {slot})` (player.js:10447) moved into the active card; measure the iOS reparent caveats
  (player.js:70-104) on WebKit headless and list what the device must confirm. Portrait items (stored width/height) fill the
  card; landscape sits centred. Progress via the existing pings (forward only, D5).
- **D8 Watch later card.** From `GET /api/watch-later` (videos and `podcast:` episodes), shown as D6 / D7 with a "Watch later"
  label; finishing removes it from the list as it does today.
- **D9 Song card.** A breather: a liked or recently played song with its art, plays the whole song, swipe skips. If v1.378.0's
  play-count route exists on main when this builds, songs report through it (and its skip rule); otherwise nothing extra.
- **D10 Length picker.** On open: 10 / 20 / 30 min (last choice preselected). A thin progress ring in the corner; no numbers
  unless tapped.
- **D11 Wind down.** At time up: the current card finishes its slice (at most 2 more minutes; a book card until moved on),
  playback stops, then the recap.
- **D12 Recap.** "20 minutes: 14 pages of <Book>, 2 chapters of <Video>, half of <Episode>, 3 songs" built from what actually
  happened (counted on the client, stored per D13). Buttons: Done (back to where he came from) and "Another 10 minutes" (a hold
  to confirm, not a tap; extensions listed in the recap and the session record).
- **D13 Session history.** A per-user table (next schema version): `user_feed_sessions(user_id, id, started_at, planned_min,
  extensions, actual_sec, summary_json)` where summary_json holds the recap counts and the D5 previous positions. Wired into
  lib/auth/store.js statements, backup export/import, the test reset, user deletion, and docs/DIAGRAMS.md (census). A small
  "This week: 1 h 20 min in Feed, 5 sessions" line on the length picker. No other stats page change.
- **D14 Out of v1.** PDFs, comics, articles, "on this day", Shorts detection, muted previews, desktop-specific layout work.
