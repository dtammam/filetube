FileTube Downloader

Point this at your own FileTube instance. Your instance must be started with FILETUBE_YTDLP_ENABLED and FILETUBE_API_TOKEN set; the token below is that value. It is stored locally in the extension and is only sent to the instance URL you configure here.

e.g. https://filetube.example.com (no trailing path).
The value of your server's FILETUBE_API_TOKEN env var.
